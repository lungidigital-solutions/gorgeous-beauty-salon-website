About the salon

A vibrant space for your beauty moments.

Gorgeous Beauty Salon by Lwazi is a welcoming destination for hair, nails and eyelashes, brought to life through a distinctive pink-and-gold identity.

Our atmosphere

Bold in colour. Warm in character.

The salon’s vivid pink interior makes a memorable first impression, balanced by gold accents and carefully arranged beauty spaces. It is an environment created to feel personal, polished and full of energy.

Our approach is simple: listen to the look you want, help you explore the available beauty options and create an experience centred on how you want to feel.

Our philosophy

Beauty should feel personal

Whether your preferred look is understated, expressive or completely new, your appointment begins with a conversation.

Your inspiration mattersBring reference photos or describe the finish you have in mind.
Details make the lookColour, shape, length and texture all help define your result.
Confidence is the goalThe best beauty experience is one that leaves you feeling like yourself.
